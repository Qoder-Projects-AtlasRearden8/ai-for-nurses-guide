Before you press send

Three rules that outrank speed.

  1. 01
    Protect the data.

    No PHI in unapproved tools. Removing a name is not de-identification.

  2. 02
    Verify the facts.

    Every clinical claim needs a source outside the model. Fluency is not evidence.

  3. 03
    Keep a human accountable.

    A named professional reviews, signs, and owns the result. AI does not inherit responsibility.

If any answer is unclear, stop and ask. See the full stop list →

Not medical advice

This is an educational guide, not a clinical protocol. It does not replace your organization’s policies, applicable law, licensing requirements, current clinical guidelines, or the judgment of a qualified professional. Do not use AI to diagnose, treat, triage, prescribe, or make decisions about a specific patient. Read the full scope and disclaimer.
